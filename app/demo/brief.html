Documentation tooling for a 40-person engineering team

Buyer: Loom Labs · Budget 1,000 credits · Delivery within 60 days · Tender closes when the buyer closes it

What we are buying

A tool that keeps our public API reference in step with the code. Today one engineer rewrites the reference by hand every release, which takes two days and still drifts.

The brief, in four requirements

  1. A public repository with the tool source, a licence, and a README that a new engineer can follow without asking us anything.
  2. A live URL that runs the tool on a sample repository, so we can watch a reference page being generated rather than read a claim that it works.
  3. A two-week plan with dates and numbers: what ships in week one, what ships in week two, how many endpoints the sample covers, and how long a full run takes.
  4. A maintenance commitment of at least six months, with a named person and a response time for a broken run.

How proposals are scored

Quality is 60% of the score and is read against this page, once, by the validators. Price is 30% and favours the cheapest bid. Delivery time is 10% and favours the fastest. The weights were fixed when the tender opened and cannot change.

What is not part of the brief

Nothing about your company size, your other clients, or a slide deck. We read the repository, the live URL and the plan. A proposal that answers a different question scores accordingly.
